# Code of Conduct

Orbit is a small, friendly project. We want everyone — contributors and users —
to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers; help people learn.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion or the project.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the issue tracker, pull requests, the
`#orbit` channel, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact a maintainer
privately (on `#orbit`, or via the maintainer's Codeberg profile). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
and may warn or ban anyone who violates this code.
